# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge
We commit to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone.

## Expected behavior
- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent.
- Focus feedback on the code, not people.

## Unacceptable behavior
- Harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.
- Doxxing or sharing private information.
- Repeated disruptive behavior.

## Enforcement
Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation for violations.

## Reporting
Report conduct concerns privately to project maintainers via security/contact channels in `SECURITY.md`.
